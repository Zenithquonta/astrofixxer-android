# Google Play listing (draft)

**App name:** AstroFixxer

**Short description** (80 characters max):
Offline star-hopping guide: strap your phone to a telescope and find any object.

**Full description:**

AstroFixxer turns your phone into a push-to guide for manual telescopes, and doubles as an offline planetarium.

Strap the phone to the telescope tube, point at a bright star, tap it to align, then pick a target. Arrows show which way to move and how far, until the target is in the eyepiece.

• Works with no internet. About 100,000 deep-sky objects and stars (from Stellarium and the HYG database), plus planets, comets and asteroids, are built into the app.
• Stellarium-style sky: atmosphere and twilight, Milky Way, landscapes, light-pollution slider, and Western and Indian constellation lines and artwork.
• Events for the next 60 days, all worked out on the phone: moon phases, eclipses, equinoxes, meteor showers, conjunctions, supermoons, planet gatherings, Mercury and Venus transits, occultations of bright stars by the Moon, bright comets, visible ISS and Tiangong passes, and ISS crossings of the Sun and Moon.
• Time travel: see the sky at any date and time.
• Your own observing lists, and your own objects added by coordinates.
• AstroGuide voice assistant in English and Hindi: "find Jupiter", "what is M31", "next meteor shower".
• Night mode turns everything red to protect your dark adaptation.
• Search by Messier, NGC, IC, Caldwell and other catalogue numbers, or by common name.

Free and open source (GPLv3). Based on AstroHopper by Artyom Beilis. Sky data from Stellarium and the HYG database.
